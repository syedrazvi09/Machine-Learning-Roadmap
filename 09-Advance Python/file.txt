Hello everyone, I’m excited to present AURORA – an AI-driven career advisor.

At its core, our prototype takes a student’s assessment or test results, automatically scores them, maps them to specific skills, and then recommends career tracks that match their strengths. For example, if a student shows high scores in Python and SQL, the system might recommend tracks like Data Analyst or Python Developer.

Right now, our prototype demonstrates the end-to-end flow:

Students input their assessment scores.

The system analyzes their skills using a Skill Genome.

It computes career fit scores across multiple roles.

And finally, it provides personalized recommendations and highlights skill gaps.

This already makes the process of career advice more data-driven and personalized than traditional one-size-fits-all counseling.

Now, what makes AURORA truly unique is where we’re headed. The future model won’t just stop at scoring. It will integrate:

Dynamic market signals, so students know which roles are rising or declining in demand.

Learning friction analysis, estimating how much effort is needed to reach each target role.

Mini-project and mentorship recommendations, so every skill gap translates into a practical, guided step for the student.

And a strong focus on fairness, transparency, and explainability, so students understand why a career path was suggested.

The finished AURORA will be like a GPS for careers: it won’t just tell you where you are, but also show multiple routes to where you want to be, with real-time updates based on the job market.

This combination of assessment-based skill profiling, real-time market awareness, and personalized, actionable guidance is what makes AURORA stand out from existing career advisory tools.
